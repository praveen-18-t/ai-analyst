# Sales Policy

## Order classification
Orders are classified as **Premium** when the order revenue is 300 USD or more after discounts.
Orders between 100 and 299.99 USD are **Standard**. Anything below 100 USD is **Basic**.

## Discounts
Discounts above 10 percent require manager approval and are only allowed for the partner channel.
Web and store orders are capped at 10 percent.

## Regions
The North and East regions are reported together as "Eastern Operations" in quarterly reviews.
South and West are reported as "Western Operations".

## Missing revenue
Orders with missing revenue are treated as pending invoices and must be excluded from revenue reporting
until finance confirms the amount.
